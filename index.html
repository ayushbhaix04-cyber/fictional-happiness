Question Format Practice

Practise common board-style formats. Reveal the key only after attempting.

Assertion–Reason

Assertion: In a series circuit, the current is the same through every component.

Reason: There is only one path for charge to flow.

Both statements are true, and the Reason correctly explains the Assertion.
Case-based question

A 6 Ω resistor is connected to a 12 V battery. Assume ideal wires and a steady temperature.

Find the current and the electrical power.

Using I = V/R = 12/6 = 2 A. Power P = VI = 12 × 2 = 24 W.
